§ 01

About

I’m a Senior Research Scientist at Zoom, where I work on LLM post-training for Zoom AI Companion, federated AI for LLM agents, and healthcare-focused language models.

I’ve been doing machine learning research for over 15 years across Zoom, Samsung Research, IBM Research and TCS Innovation Labs. That work has covered question answering, dialogue systems, reading comprehension, neural machine translation, end-to-end and simultaneous speech translation, meeting summarization, and more recently alignment, reward modeling and agentic reinforcement learning for LLMs.

At Samsung I started the speech translation effort and led an NLP research team. Our reading comprehension models ranked 2nd on TriviaQA, 3rd on MS MARCO and 10th on SQuAD, and our meta-learning and multi-task methods improved speech translation across many language pairs. At Zoom, I built the models behind live meeting translation and LLM-based meeting summaries.

I studied Computer Science at the Indian Institute of Science (M.E., Machine Learning), where my thesis on quasi-Newton methods for semi-supervised classification received the highest dissertation grade. I review for and serve as an Area Chair at AAAI, ACL and EMNLP.

Research areas

Most of my work sits where modeling research meets products used by millions of people.

01

LLM post-training & alignment

Preference optimization, reward modeling, instruction tuning and distillation, so models follow intent faithfully and safely.

WPO · ISE · Hybrid rewards · TCIA · KD in post-training

02

Agents & reasoning

Reinforcement learning for multi-turn, tool-using and collaborative agents: credit assignment, checklist rewards, and stress-test benchmarks.

RELACE · CM2 · LiveMCP-101 · LogicIF · C2C

03

Multilingual LLMs

Natural, diverse multilingual instruction data, cross-lingual evaluation, and verifiable rewards for reasoning beyond English.

Multilingual IFT · Cross-lingual eval · Verifiable semantics

04

Speech & simultaneous translation

End-to-end speech-to-text translation with meta-learning, multi-task learning, monotonic attention and robustness to noisy input.

MAML-ST · TAMTL · LM-augmented MMA · CLAD-ST

05

Summarization, QA & dialogue

Meeting summarization through LLM distillation, reading comprehension over long documents, and question generation from knowledge graphs.

Select-Prompt-Filter · Context Zoom-in · KG question generation

06

Healthcare & federated AI

Adapting LLMs to clinical settings, and federated learning approaches that keep LLM agents useful without centralizing sensitive data.
